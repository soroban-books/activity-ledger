Activity Ledger
Runs on your machine. Reads public chain data only.

Turn a Stellar address into books

Decodes Soroban DeFi and classic activity, prices it, tracks tax lots and lending interest, and exports for Koinly or your accountant.

One or more addresses you own, separated by spaces or new lines. Transfers between them are treated as non-taxable.

Decoding is slow today, roughly 5 seconds of CPU per ledger. Try 20 transactions first. Syncing again continues where it stopped. Older activity than Horizon's rolling window (about a year) cannot be discovered.
